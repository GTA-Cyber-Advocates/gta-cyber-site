Get involved

Three ways in.

Whether you practise security, want to support the community, or are not yet part of Global Tech Advocates at all.

Practitioners

Join the community

You work in cybersecurity and you are already in a GTA network, or you would like to be. Membership is free. What we ask is that you show up occasionally and answer a question when you are the right person to answer it.

Organisations

Partner or sponsor

Fund a strand of the programme, host a roundtable, or release a practitioner’s time. The terms are on the partners page, including everything sponsorship deliberately does not buy.

Everyone else

Ask us something

You have a security question, a policy consultation with a deadline, or an idea for the community. The form reaches a person rather than a queue.

Say hello

Tell us what you are after

We read everything and reply to anything that is not obviously automated.

Or email info@gta-cyber.org directly. We do not add anyone to a mailing list without asking, and we do not pass details to sponsors.

Not in Global Tech Advocates yet?

GTA membership is free and open to anyone in or adjacent to the tech ecosystem. Join a network near you first, then come back to us. Or write to us and we will point you at the right network.
