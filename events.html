Events

Sessions, roundtables and the occasional bigger thing.

The community runs small and often rather than large and rare. Most sessions are practitioner-only and held under Chatham House rules so people can speak plainly.

Programme

What we run

A rhythm rather than a calendar of set pieces. Dates are announced to members and on LinkedIn as each one is confirmed.

  • Monthly

    Practitioner call

    One hour, video, no slides required. Someone brings a problem or a post-incident lesson, the room works on it. Open to members practising in security.

  • Quarterly

    Deep-dive roundtable

    A single subject examined properly with people who have done it: post-quantum migration planning, supply chain assurance, securing an organisation with no security team.

  • As needed

    Policy response sessions

    Convened when a consultation with a real deadline lands. Short, focused, and aimed at producing evidence that is actually submitted rather than a discussion that is merely had.

  • Alongside GTA

    Contributions to network events

    Where another GTA group is running something and wants a security strand, the community supplies speakers and content rather than staging a competing event.

Hosting and speaking

Offer a room or a topic

Two things we always need: practitioners willing to open a session with something real, and organisations willing to host a roundtable.

Hosting means a room and coffee, not sponsorship. Speaking means twenty honest minutes on something you have actually done, not a polished keynote. Neither is a sales opportunity, and sessions where somebody tries to make one tend not to get a second invitation.
